- Baby toy music box lullaby — SFX 700

The city ambience remains in the project for provenance but is not loaded by the current experience. The music-box track fades out before the rain finale, which uses the owner-supplied rain and thunder derivatives plus the public-domain fireworks derivatives listed below.

The following private, non-commercial derivatives were produced from media supplied directly by the site owner. The source MP4 files remain outside the project and were not modified:

- `rain-real-loop-v1.mp3` — source `雨声.mp4`, 14:00–15:00 stable window; 60-second stereo derivative, loudness-normalized, 1.1-second entrance fade and loop-edge fade.
- `thunder-real-v1.mp3` — source `雷声.mp4`, beginning at 01:04.55; 8.4-second derivative including the pre-strike onset and decay, loudness-normalized and faded.
- `firework-real-v1.mp3` — source `烟花.mp4`, beginning at 03:47.00; retained for provenance but no longer loaded because this source window contains background music and vocals.

The three active firework sounds are derived from **Fireworks in distance - 1**, recorded by ezwa in the Belgian countryside and released into the public domain by its author. Source and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fireworks_in_distance_1.ogg

- `firework-distance-main-v2.mp3` — 06.40–11.20 derivative; primary distant launch, burst, and decay.
- `firework-distance-left-v2.mp3` — 12.90–17.40 derivative; separate left-shell burst and decay.
- `firework-distance-right-v2.mp3` — 18.60–23.10 derivative; separate right-shell burst and decay.

The complete 66.7-second source and all three derivatives were checked with a medium speech-recognition model plus voice-activity filtering; no lexical speech or lyrics were detected in the replacement source or derivatives.

`umbrella-pov-real-v1.png` is an original AI-generated project asset created for this private site, then locally chroma-keyed to transparency. It is not sourced from a third-party stock library.

`gift-box-photo-v2.png` and `gift-box-photo-open-v2.png` are original AI-generated project assets created as a matched closed/open product-photography pair for the text-free gift interaction, then locally chroma-keyed to transparency. They are not sourced from a third-party stock library.

`storm-cloud-bank-v1.png` is an original AI-generated cloud-only texture created for the Three.js rain alley. `storm-alley-backplate-v1.png` is an original AI-generated lightning-free distant-alley backplate used behind real 3D foreground geometry. `secret-heart-v1.png` is an original AI-generated lacquer-heart entry mark, locally chroma-keyed to transparency. None of these assets is sourced from a third-party stock library.
